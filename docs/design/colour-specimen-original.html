paint-a-pint / colour specimen

The original 14-token palette, kept as the palette of record (see DESIGN.md). A later trimmed 10-token version and one-accent schemes live in colour-specimen.html. Docs only, not bundled by Vite. Click any swatch to copy its hex. Fonts load from Google Fonts, so you need to be online.

How the sampling works. I clustered the pixels of 32 images (your paintings, desk photos, and the room inspo) in Lab colour space, keeping only the colourful pixels so walls and shadows don't drown the result. The percentages are each colour's share of those colourful pixels. These are phone photos and screenshots, so white balance and compression shift things: treat them as direction, not gospel.

4 · Contrast: what may be used as text

Computed live in this page (WCAG 2.x). Small text needs 4.5:1. The rule that falls out: pink, green and sky are fills and tape, never small text on light surfaces. Put the label on cream next to the colour, or use terracotta / ultramarine for coloured text.

6 · Decisions for you

The three places where the samples don't force an answer.

1 · the green

Keep the current cool mint (it matches the inspo chair), or shift to the yellower leaf green your paintings and window trees use? Leaf is the truer match to the photos; mint is already in the app and reads friendlier next to the pink.

2 · a second night surface

Warm near-black only, or also the dusk indigo for a menu / evening mood? Dusk is the strongest single colour in the inspo, but pink text on it is weak (3.8:1).

3 · do rooms recolour the UI?

One fixed UI palette (simpler, consistent), or the accent pair shifts per room (section 5)? Either is cheap now and costly to retrofit.
