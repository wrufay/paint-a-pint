paint-a-pint / colour specimen

Reference page for choosing the project colours (docs only, not bundled by Vite). Click any swatch to copy its hex. Fonts load from Google Fonts, so you need to be online.

How the sampling works. I clustered the pixels of 32 images (your paintings, desk photos, and the room inspo) in Lab colour space, keeping only the colourful pixels so walls and shadows don't drown the result. The percentages are each colour's share of those colourful pixels. These are phone photos and screenshots, so white balance and compression shift things: treat them as direction, not gospel.

Update: the palette of record is the original 14-token one (see DESIGN.md and colour-specimen-original.html). This page keeps the trimmed 10-token version, the one-accent schemes and the night-mode exploration as alternatives, not the decision.

4 · Contrast: what may be used as text

Computed live in this page (WCAG 2.x). Small text needs 4.5:1. The rule that falls out: pink and mint green are fills, borders and tape, never small text on light surfaces. For coloured text or a filled button on light, use --green-deep.

5 · Decisions and parked colours

One decision left in the palette itself, plus everything I cut, kept here so it isn't lost.

1 · the green's hue

Keep the cool mint (it's in the app and matches the inspo chair), or shift to the yellower leaf green your paintings and window trees use? If you pick leaf, --green-deep gets re-derived from it. Mint is friendlier next to the pink; leaf is the truer match to the photos.

2 · how deep is green-deep

I picked #357059: the lightest shade that passes 4.5:1 on both cream and paper. #3a7d64 is brighter but drops to 4.26:1 on paper.

Parked: sampled, not tokensrevisit if rooms recolour the UI

If you later want per-room accents (alps: sky + ultramarine; home: terracotta + rose) or an evening menu (dusk), these are the sampled starting points. Adding them later is cheap; carrying them now would mean extra accents with no current use.

6 · Schemes and night mode

A scheme is one accent (or, for "fruit salad", the strawberry + melon pair with fixed jobs: pink is tape and decoration, green does every action). A mode is the surface: light (cream), night (a deep indigo taken from the inspo), or dark (your existing warm near-black). Pick any combination below; the board uses the app's real pieces: the dashed card border, taped chips, slider, and the selected-swatch ring.

The sampled dusk indigo #404070 is too light to carry accents: strawberry falls to 3.8:1, melon to 3.4:1, blueberry to 3.5:1. So night mode uses two darker siblings, #232340 for the page and #30305a for cards. On both, plus your warm dark, every night accent passes 4.5:1. The chip and tape stay paper-coloured in every mode because they're physical objects.
